# 13. Important Content Guidelines

The landing page should only claim services and capabilities supported by the provided company information.

Do not add services such as:

- Visa processing
- Emirates ID services
- PRO services
- Immigration services
- Legal services
- Document attestation
- Government transaction services beyond the licensed document-clearing activity

unless the company separately confirms that these services are offered.

The website copy should communicate:

- Professionalism
- Reliability
- Simplicity
- Convenience
- Dubai presence
- Documentation expertise

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- "Dubai's #1 document clearing company"
- "The best document clearing company"
- "Guaranteed approval"
- "Fastest service in Dubai"
- "100% success rate"

unless the company can substantiate them.
